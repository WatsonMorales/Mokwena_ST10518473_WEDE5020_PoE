ABOUT SOLE REVIVE

We Bring Your Favourite Shoes Back to Life.

We at SOLE REVIVE are a Mamelodi-based shoe cleaning and care business focused on giving you professional, reliable and quality-focused footwear cleaning services.

Our Story

SOLE REVIVE was created to everyone with a convenient and professional solution for keeping their favourite kicks clean and well taken care of. We understand that shoes are an important part of personal style and obviously of everyday life, which is why we are so centered on giving every pair the attention it deserves.

Our goal is to combine local accessibility in our beloved neighbbourhood with a premium customer experience, allowing customers in Mamelodi and surrounding areas to access our services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide good quality shoe cleaning services while delivering excellent customer service to all our beloved supporters while still pauing maximum attention to detail.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised shoe-care brand in Mamelodi and surrounding communities, known for quality, professionalism and customer satisfaction.

Our Values

  • Quality: We aim on delivering our reliable and professional cleaning abilities.
  • Attention to Detail: Each and Every pair receives careful attention all the way through the cleaning process.
  • Customer Satisfaction: We shoot to provide a to better our customers' satisfaction from enquiry to collection.
  • Professionalism: We keep a very professional (with sprinkles of fun here and there) approach to our services and customer interactions.
  • Community: We are incredibly proud to provide a local service to our customers in Mamelodi, and ofcourse we value our community deeply.
