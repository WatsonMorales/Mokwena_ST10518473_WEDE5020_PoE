ABOUT SOLE REVIVE

We Bring Your Favourite Shoes Back to Life.

SOLE REVIVE is a Mamelodi-based shoe cleaning and care business focused on providing professional, reliable and quality-focused footwear cleaning services.

Our Story

SOLE REVIVE was created to provide customers with a convenient and professional solution for keeping their favourite footwear clean and well maintained. We understand that shoes are an important part of personal style and everyday life, which is why we focus on giving every pair the attention it deserves.

Our goal is to combine local accessibility with a premium customer experience, allowing customers in Mamelodi and surrounding areas to access quality shoe-care services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality shoe cleaning and care services while delivering excellent customer service and attention to detail.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised shoe-care brand in Mamelodi and surrounding communities, known for quality, professionalism and customer satisfaction.

Our Values

  • Quality: We focus on delivering reliable and professional cleaning services.
  • Attention to Detail: Every pair receives careful attention throughout the cleaning process.
  • Customer Satisfaction: We aim to provide a positive experience from enquiry to collection.
  • Professionalism: We maintain a professional approach to our services and customer interactions.
  • Community: We are proud to provide a local service to customers in Mamelodi and surrounding areas.

Why Choose SOLE REVIVE?

Choosing SOLE REVIVE means choosing a service that values your footwear and your experience. Our focus on quality, attention to detail and customer service allows us to provide a professional alternative to cleaning your shoes yourself.
